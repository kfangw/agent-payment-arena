# Today's payment sizes (design fixed before the run)

Scripts: `scripts/run_H.py` (check / smoke / run), `scripts/analyze_H.py` (metrics, paired block bootstrap, reading rules). Results: `results/exposure_law/` (summary.json, plan/, checks, log). Per-law block-sum files (6 npz, 64 MB) are not included; `run_H.py run` regenerates them in about 20 minutes on two cores.

World: E-fitted, flow F2, the main run's constants and its compiled table at the true constants (not recompiled). Only the exposure law changes: six clipped log-normal laws, L0 (main run: median $30, sigma 1, [0.5, 2000]); L1 (mean 0.20, sigma 1, [0.01, 2000]); L2 (mean 0.20, sigma 2); L3 (mean 0.20, sigma 4.0789, the unclipped fit to a mean of 0.20 and 95% of value at or above $1; clipping makes its realized mean 0.136, so it is a sensitivity only); L4 (mean 1, sigma 1); L5 (mean 5, sigma 1). Evaluation: seed 111, 5,663,400 payments per law in 113,268 blocks, same path for all policies. Policies: Comp (the table); Cutoff (grant iff pi0 < 0.2552 at arrival, never waits or asks); B4_L (the two-threshold watch rule retuned on 200,000 payments of law L, default_rng([111, 700, L])); B4_L0 (the L0-tuned B4 carried over). Payments below the table's lowest exposure row ($0.50) read that row; direct backward induction at $0.01, $0.05, $0.20 and $0.50 gives the same first action at every belief point and both arrival regimes (0 mismatches).

Reading rules fixed in advance: (a) if at L1 Comp minus Cutoff is within 10 bp of L1's mean exposure, the main text says the policy is that one-threshold rule at today's sizes; (b) value share above v* under L2 and L3 is reported, and the "most value lies where the policy acts" statement is made only if the L3 share is at least 50%; (c) the smallest mean in the sweep at which the gain exceeds the margin is reported.

Result: (a) +3.2 bp [2.5, 4.0] at L1; (b) 35% (L2), 81% (L3, sensitivity); (c) $1 (47.5 bp [44.9, 49.8]). Comp minus B4_L at small means is a grid artifact (the tuner's grid enforces a < b and cannot express a pure cutoff), so the Cutoff is the comparator there.
